That shouldn't be a problem. A computer game is a child of many parents, each with their own style and habits. I'm sure everyone misses the mark once in a while. If that happened to me, I'd want to know why my work was rejected and have a chance to discuss the reasons behind it. First, I'd need to understand what was wrong so I could avoid making the same mistake next time. Second, I believe that in a creative environment, there should be room to defend my creative choices. But in the end, when all is said and done, if my work is considered unsuitable, I'll rewrite it without mercy.
