Daniel Konc
Interactive Interview

Here's the thing. When creating a detective story, I'm afraid of making it too easy for the players, so I tend to give them less information than they need. It's a long-term problem I'm actively trying to overcome. So in recent years, I've been deliberately giving players more information than I'm comfortable with. Like a personal detox. Of course, other people's perspectives can help me calibrate the number of clues and find the right balance. So yes, it's challenging, but no tears of despair have been shed.
