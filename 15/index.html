Interactive Interview

Although I don't have any published books or longer works of fiction, there are some texts I'd like to present. The first is this interactive interview itself. It was created specifically for this application to CDPR, and its purpose is to demonstrate my ability to handle branching dialogue and let the reader discover different information on each playthrough depending on the questions they choose. I tried to make it feel at least somewhat like an in-game dialogue.

The second group consists of texts I've been writing for many years for a small informal writing challenge on a Czech RPG website. Each entry is defined by a theme and a maximum length of 250 words. The form is entirely up to the writer. Because the format is so short, it mainly rewards strong ideas and surprising twists. I often use it to experiment with different genres and writing styles.

The third is a description of a fictional city called Karakert. It's pre-game material for the players in my RPG campaign, describing the setting of the story. Worldbuilding. I try to build it in layers, with attention to causes and consequences. The individual hooks in the text are deliberately left vague. The idea is to give the players something to build on themselves. I find that more interesting than serving them everything fully defined and leaving no room for their own imagination. This way, they may (or may not) take some of those hooks and make them their own.
