Interactive Interview

Of course, I understand that I won't be responsible for the main story (I wouldn't mind), but what I'm interested in is creating story content. Quests, NPCs, lore, history, background content, or anything else I've forgotten to mention that belongs to worldbuilding and shaping the main character's journey through the story. Stories have been my main hobby throughout my life, and it doesn't matter whether I tell them or just consume them. I believe storytelling is one of my talents, as is working closely with a team, because years of experience have shown me that human interaction is what inspires me most. In addition to developing stories, I'd also like to be involved in writing the actual text, because writing is the most natural way for me to get a thought from my head onto the screen in the form I want it to take. Of course, I can write for already established content, but what I enjoy most is the whole creative process: from a small initial idea, through figuring out how it should work, to bringing it into its final written form.
