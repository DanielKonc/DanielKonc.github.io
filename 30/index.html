Throughout my life, I've worked in many different fields and done many different things. Most of those jobs gave me satisfaction and the pleasure of a job well done. But they weren't THE right thing. Just an exchange of my time for money.

At the same time, I've known people lucky enough to have a job they genuinely enjoy. Love, even. Just as I love storytelling and writing, two of my lifelong passions. Those people showed me the difference between doing something simply out of obligation and doing it with genuine enthusiasm, even though both can be hard work.

For a long time, the possibility of earning a living doing something I loved simply never occurred to me. And later, when it did occur to me, the cards had already been dealt. Until now. I just happen to be between jobs, which has given me time to reconsider where I want to go next. I've wanted to do this for a long time, but the timing was never right — either I had a young child to think about or a job satisfying enough that quitting it just to see what happened didn't make much sense.

Right now, the road ahead is clear, and for once I can choose which direction to take. I'd really like that road to lead across the border to the north-east.
