Interactive Interview

I consider dialogue the hardest part of writing. For example, when I read a book, my enjoyment is directly proportional to the quality of its dialogue and how well it is used. That's why I practise it. And that's hard because I have plenty of time for every response. Much more than I would during a real conversation. And all that extra time results in extremely thoughtful and clever answers. Which is fundamentally wrong. For dialogue to feel authentic, you need to get into the characters' mindsets and write what they would actually say if they had only a fraction of the time to come up with it, rather than giving them the cleverest line I could come up with after thinking about it for a week.
