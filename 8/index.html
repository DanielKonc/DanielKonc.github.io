I've been working hard since I was fifteen. In retail, in assistance services — both are jobs where there is always more work to be done than time to actually do it. I'm not afraid of deadlines as long as a brutal period is followed by a more relaxed one. That I can handle. I also understand that game development can be difficult in terms of the work itself. Routine writing, corrections, throwing out things you've already written and starting from scratch, frustration, time pressure. I imagine nobody looks forward to those things, but what I'm trying to say is that everything is more bearable if you enjoy the work and like being there.
