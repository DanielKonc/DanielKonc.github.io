Daniel Konc
Interactive Interview

I don't have an overly romanticised idea of what the job looks like. First, I imagine there would be a basic structure — the skeleton of the main story. Then the world in which it takes place, the main characters, their relationships, and their connections to the story. After that, I imagine it being broken down into smaller and smaller pieces that need to be filled with content: quests, characters, history, points of interest, and so on. All the while, I'd keep refining the main story, checking that the characters and their decisions remain consistent with who they are and the situations they're in, and gradually bringing all the pieces together into a coherent whole.

So I imagine a storyteller's work as being given a small piece of the world and being asked to breathe life into it. Alternatively, if CDPR divides the work by type of content rather than by location, then I imagine I might work on a particular kind of content — quests, points of interest, characters, for example — across different parts of the game. At the same time, I'd constantly compare what I create with the existing material and the overall concept of the world to make sure everything fits together.
