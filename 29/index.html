Yes, it's a very specific discipline. But I haven't had a problem with it in the past, so I don't expect it to be a problem in the future either. When I helped create an adventure for an RPG competition, it was a collaborative effort involving several writers. There was one main author, and the rest of us had to fit our work into his overall vision. On top of that, I love rules and like following them. So if they're set from the start, I'll stick to them with the tenacity of a bulldog.

I also consider it an advantage that I feel completely at home in the Witcher universe. On the other hand, if I encountered something that seemed to deviate from the established concept, I'd probably bring it up to check whether everything was as intended.
