Because game development is a team effort, I'd love to work on-site, at least partially. Writing for a game is an organic process that doesn't happen in isolation, and every change can affect the work of many other teams. That's why I find the idea of being in the office and discussing potential problems directly with the people responsible for them very appealing. At the same time, having other people around helps me concentrate. I find it easier to articulate my thoughts and come up with new ideas when I can talk things through with someone. And finally, being on-site would help me better understand the structure of game development and the roles of the different teams in the overall process.
