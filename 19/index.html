Daniel Konc
Interactive Interview

There are two areas of storytelling that I believe I excel at. Now, I don't want to sound arrogant, but if I weren't convinced that I had something to contribute even in a team as experienced as yours, I wouldn't be trying.

First, I excel at auditing the overall story structure. I can take an already established structure apart and find logical flaws, problems with characters' motivations, and inconsistencies in their behaviour. I identify the problems and fix them. I really dislike stories where the antagonist has to suddenly act like a fool just so they can lose. The whole structure, including the climax, should hold together even if every side of the conflict does its best to pursue its goals in a way that makes sense given the situation they're in.

My second strong suit is expanding an existing premise. Give me a basic idea, and I can broaden and deepen it, adding new layers. I can figure out the why and the how, and build a story structure around that initial premise. Those are my two storytelling superpowers, and I'm genuinely proud of them.
