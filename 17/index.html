Although I'm not a professional writer, I do have a certain sense of professional integrity. So I consider using AI to rewrite or creatively improve my writing, or to fish for ideas unethical. I only use chatbots to look up terms or meanings. I also use AI to help me translate texts into English, because while I'm comfortable speaking and understanding English, my written English still has some gaps, and AI helps me bridge them.
